Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 25, 2025

Version: 1.0

Language: English

CarCat LLC ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the CarCat mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect
1.1 Personal Information

We may collect the following personal information:

- Name
- Phone number
- Email address
- Profile photo (optional)
1.2 Vehicle Information

We may collect vehicle-related information, including:

- Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)
- License plate number
- Brand, model, and year
- Engine type and transmission
- Current and historical mileage
- Vehicle photos (optional)
1.3 Service History Data

We may collect service-related data, such as:

- Bookings and appointments
- Service dates and service types
- Maintenance schedules and records
1.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

- Location or GPS data
- Payment or card information
- Browsing history
- Biometric information
2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for the following purposes:

- Creating and managing user accounts
- Registering and managing vehicles
- Facilitating service bookings
- Tracking service and maintenance history
- Sending maintenance reminders and notifications
- Responding to support requests
- Improving application features and performance
- Fixing technical issues
3. How We Store Your Information
3.1 Data Storage

We do not store data locally on your device. All information is:

- Stored securely on our servers
- Encrypted during transmission using HTTPS/TLS
- Protected using industry-standard security measures
- Regularly backed up
3.2 Data Retention
- Active accounts: Data is retained while the account remains active
- After account deletion: Data is permanently deleted within 90 days
4. Data Sharing and Disclosure
4.1 Service Providers

When you book an appointment with a service provider, we share only the information necessary to complete the booking, such as:

- Your name and phone number
- Relevant vehicle details
- Service history (if required for the service)
4.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect our legal rights, comply with legal obligations, or respond to lawful requests from authorities.

5. Firebase Cloud Messaging

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver:

- Service reminders
- Booking confirmations
- Maintenance notifications
- Application updates

You may disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

At present, push notifications are not actively used, but the infrastructure is in place.

6. Your Data Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

### Access

You may view all your personal and vehicle information within the app at any time.

### Correction

You may update or correct your information directly through the app.

### Deletion

You may delete vehicles in the app or request full account deletion by contacting us. Account data will be deleted within 90 days.

### Data Portability

You may request a copy of your data in a machine-readable format.

7. Data Security

We apply technical and organizational safeguards, including:

- Encryption of data in transit
- Secure API authentication
- Regular security reviews
- Protection against unauthorized access

You are responsible for:

- Keeping your login credentials confidential
- Using strong and unique passwords
- Logging out from shared devices
- Reporting suspicious or unauthorized activity
8. Children's Privacy

CarCat does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without parental consent.

Users under 18:

- Must use CarCat with parental or guardian guidance
- May register vehicles on behalf of family members
- Remain under the responsibility of their parents or legal guardians
9. Third-Party Services
### Google ML Kit

CarCat uses Google ML Kit for VIN scanning functionality. VIN processing occurs entirely on the user's device and scanned data is not transmitted to Google.

### Firebase Cloud Messaging

Firebase may collect limited device identifiers solely for notification delivery.

### No Other Third Parties

We do not use analytics platforms, advertising networks, social media integrations, or payment processors.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When updates occur:

- The "Last Updated" date will be revised
- Users will be notified through the app or via email
- Continued use of CarCat constitutes acceptance of the updated policy
